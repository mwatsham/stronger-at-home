# Delicate At-Home Physiotherapy Logo Design

Status: Approved raster-only design; awaiting written-spec review.

## Objective

Use the supplied home-physiotherapy artwork as a coherent symbol that communicates three ideas:

1. physiotherapy is delivered inside the patient's home;
2. mobility improves through gradual, supported progression; and
3. the service feels reassuring, personal, and capable.

The approved concept study is stored at `assets/home-physiotherapy-logo-approved-concept-v2.png`. It is the immutable raster artwork source for the primary logo lockup. Do not trace, redraw, regenerate, recolour, remove its pale background or glow, or create an SVG version.

## Source asset treatment

- Preserve the complete source PNG unchanged at 1254 × 1254 pixels.
- Crop only excess outer pale space when composing the primary lockup.
- Cropping must be rectangular and must not clip the visible artwork or glow.
- Retain a visually even pale margin around the roof, side walls and supporting hand.
- Do not apply background removal, transparency, sharpening, colour correction, filters or AI editing.

## Approved composition

### Home

- An open deep-blue house outline shelters the figure and steps.
- The outline consists of a shallow pitched roof, a small chimney, and two short side walls.
- The house remains open at the bottom so it feels welcoming rather than enclosed.
- The vertical roof-to-head clearance is no more than one-and-a-half figure-head diameters, while retaining an intentional pale gap.
- The roof and walls use rounded caps and joins with one consistent line weight.

### Figure and progression

- A deep-blue monoline figure walks upward inside the house.
- The figure uses the same rounded outline language as the house and hand: outlined circular head, smooth torso, and rounded limbs.
- The pose and monoline treatment in the supplied raster are approved and must remain unchanged.
- Exactly three short, slim warm-gold steps rise from left to right.
- The raised front foot sits directly above the lowest step with a narrow pale gap and no contact.
- The figure and steps read as one centred activity group with balanced space to the roof and side walls.

### Supporting hand

- A single cupped deep-blue hand supports the home without touching it.
- The hand's outline bounding box spans 88–92% of the house outline's width, making it slightly smaller while retaining visual support.
- The palm uses a light, flowing outline rather than a solid silhouette.
- Four long, tapered fingers have graduated lengths, natural curvature, and visible negative space between them.
- The fingers must not merge, cross, double, or appear claw-like.
- The hand is centred beneath the home with a compact but clearly pale gap.

## Spatial balance

- The complete symbol has a compact, near-square footprint.
- The house, therapy activity, and hand share one vertical centre.
- Left and right outer margins are visually balanced.
- The house aligns over the usable width of the palm and does not overpower it.
- Internal spacing follows a consistent rhythm: controlled roof headroom, even side margins around the activity, a narrow foot-to-step gap, and a compact house-to-hand gap.
- No two conceptual elements touch or overlap.

## Primary raster lockup

- Place the cropped symbol to the left of the existing `Stronger at Home Physiotherapy` wordmark.
- Keep the endorsement `by Melanie Watsham` beneath the service name.
- Use Source Serif 4 for the two wordmark lines and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for the endorsement.
- Render all wordmark text in approved Deep Navy `#203E55`.
- Extend the source image's pale edge colour across the complete lockup canvas so the cropped symbol does not appear as a separate square.
- Keep the symbol and wordmark optically centred with clear separation; neither may overlap the other.
- The raster lockup remains proposed until Melanie Watsham explicitly approves the exact final pixels.

## Visual character

- Delicate, reassuring, supportive, calm, and professional.
- Rounded linework rather than heavy filled silhouettes or sharp corners.
- Primary deep blue: `#203E55`, used for the house, figure, and hand.
- Progress accent warm gold: `#C3A26E`, used only for the three steps.
- Preserve the supplied pale background and subtle glow exactly; do not add further gradients, shadows, textures or decorative effects.
- Do not add leaves, medical crosses, arrows or decorative symbols.

## Acceptance criteria

- The home cue is immediately recognisable without dominating the hand or therapy activity.
- The supplied symbol geometry, colours, pale background and glow remain visually unchanged after cropping and placement.
- The hand, house, figure, and all three steps remain visually distinct in the 512-pixel lockup.
- The monoline figure remains recognisable as a person walking upward in the 512-pixel lockup.
- The figure's raised front foot aligns above the lowest step with a pale gap and no overlap.
- The roof retains visible headroom but does not create an empty upper band.
- The four hand fingers remain individually legible and naturally proportioned.
- The wordmark and endorsement remain legible at both required widths.
- The cropped symbol has an even pale margin and no clipped glow or artwork.
- Both exports have opaque pale backgrounds, contain no watermark and open without missing assets.
- No SVG or transparent derivative exists in the production output set.
