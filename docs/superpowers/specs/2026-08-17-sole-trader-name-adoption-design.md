# Sole-trader name adoption design

Date: 2026-08-17  
Decision owner: Project sponsor  
Status: approved direction; implementation pending

## Objective

Allow Melanie Watsham to proceed as a sole trader using `Stronger at Home
Physiotherapy` and the `Stronger@Home` display wordmark without applying for a
UK registered trade mark at this stage.

## Decision

The physiotherapy descriptor is accepted as sufficient practical
differentiation for the current business. The name will therefore be approved
for operational and public use within the defined physiotherapy offer.

The business identity remains `Melanie Watsham trading as Stronger at Home
Physiotherapy`. Official paperwork must show Melanie Watsham's name and the
trading name and must not imply that the business is incorporated.

## Trade-mark boundary

UKIPO registration is deferred. This is a commercial risk decision, not a
legal opinion, UKIPO clearance or confirmation that no third party can object.
The preliminary UKIPO findings remain part of the permanent project record.

The business must not:

- describe the name or logo as a registered trade mark;
- use the registered trade mark symbol, `®`; or
- claim exclusive UK trade-mark rights arising from registration.

The decision should be reviewed if the business receives an objection,
materially expands beyond physiotherapy, licenses the brand, changes ownership,
or decides that registered protection is commercially worthwhile.

## Brand-system changes

Implementation will:

1. mark the name as approved for sole-trader physiotherapy use;
2. record UKIPO registration as deferred with residual risk accepted;
3. remove trade-mark review as a launch blocker;
4. retain website readiness and verified credential wording as separate launch
   gates; and
5. preserve the original screening report and add the later commercial decision
   without rewriting the historical evidence.

No visual artwork, logo geometry, contact information, website deployment or
credential claim will change.

## Validation

Update the existing brand-context test for the approved operational status,
then run the complete unit-test suite and brand validator. Review the final diff
to confirm that the existing `AGENTS.md` changes and all files under `sources/`
remain untouched.
