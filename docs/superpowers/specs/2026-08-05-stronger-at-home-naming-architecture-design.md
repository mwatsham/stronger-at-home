# Stronger at Home Naming Architecture Design

Status: Approved direction; implementation and public use remain clearance-gated.

## Objective

Establish one consistent naming system for Melanie Watsham's sole-trader
physiotherapy business. The system must preserve the warmth and memorability of
`Stronger@Home`, make the clinical service immediately clear, and identify the
sole trader correctly on official business material.

## Approved architecture

### Legal identity

- Legal person: **Melanie Watsham**.
- Business structure: **sole trader**.
- The brand must not use `Limited`, `Ltd`, or wording that implies an incorporated
  company.

### Formal trading name

- Use **Stronger at Home Physiotherapy** as the written-out trading name.
- Use the written-out name on invoices, letters, contracts, policies, consent
  material, directory records, accessibility text, search metadata, and other
  contexts where clarity or accurate text interpretation matters.
- Official paperwork must identify **Melanie Watsham trading as Stronger at Home
  Physiotherapy** and include the other information required for that document.

### Customer-facing wordmark

- Style the principal brand expression as **Stronger@Home**.
- Retain **Physiotherapy** as a clearly visible descriptor in the primary lockup.
- Retain **by Melanie Watsham** as the endorsement in the primary lockup.
- The `@` is a visual styling device. It does not create a separate formal name
  and must not be used to imply distinct legal or trademark rights.
- Do not remove the Physiotherapy descriptor from the primary identity. Without
  it, the name could be mistaken for fitness, care, mobility, or home-support
  services.

The intended primary wording is:

```text
Stronger@Home
Physiotherapy
by Melanie Watsham
```

## Digital naming

- Preferred primary domain: **stronger-at-home.co.uk**.
- The domain was reported as unregistered in a Nominet lookup on 2026-08-04.
  This is a time-limited observation, not ownership or a reservation.
- Do not print, publish, or build around the domain until registration is
  complete and control has been verified.
- Use the written phrase `Stronger at Home Physiotherapy` in page titles,
  headings where search clarity matters, image alternative text, structured
  data, and social profile descriptions.
- Never expect a user to type `@` as part of the web address.

## Spoken and written usage

- Say the name as **“Stronger at Home Physiotherapy.”**
- In normal prose, write **Stronger at Home Physiotherapy**.
- Use **Stronger@Home** only as the deliberate branded display treatment or in
  a short social handle where the platform permits it.
- Do not alternate casually between `at`, `@`, and hyphenated versions in body
  copy.

## Artwork impact

- The existing exact approved PNG lockups remain immutable approval records.
- Implementing this naming decision requires new raster lockup revisions because
  the approved files currently show `Stronger at Home`, not `Stronger@Home`.
- Do not overwrite, redraw, trace, recolour, or convert the approved raster
  source artwork to SVG.
- Reuse the approved symbol pixels unchanged and revise only the typeset wordmark
  in new versioned raster outputs.
- The revised 2048-pixel and 512-pixel lockups require fresh visual review and
  explicit approval from Melanie Watsham.

## Clearance boundary

- The trading name and styled wordmark remain proposed for public use until an
  official UK Intellectual Property Office search covers exact and confusingly
  similar marks in relevant service classes.
- A Companies House result or an unregistered domain does not provide trademark
  clearance.
- The `@` symbol must be treated as equivalent in meaning to the word `at` during
  clearance.
- The domain should be registered promptly if the name proceeds, but registration
  must not be presented as evidence of rights in the name.

## Acceptance criteria

- Customers can immediately identify the service as physiotherapy delivered at
  home.
- The primary wordmark reads `Stronger@Home` with `Physiotherapy` visible.
- Formal and accessible uses spell out `Stronger at Home Physiotherapy`.
- Official paperwork identifies Melanie Watsham as the sole trader.
- The brand never implies that the business is a limited company.
- The approved symbol artwork remains pixel-identical in any revised lockup.
- New lockups are versioned separately and are not treated as approved until
  Melanie Watsham approves the exact files.
- Public use remains blocked until name, trademark, domain, credential, contact,
  and source-artwork requirements are recorded as resolved.

## Decision

The project sponsor selected this architecture as Option 1 on 2026-08-05:

- formal trading name: **Stronger at Home Physiotherapy**;
- styled wordmark: **Stronger@Home** with **Physiotherapy**;
- preferred domain: **stronger-at-home.co.uk**; and
- official identity: **Melanie Watsham trading as Stronger at Home
  Physiotherapy**.
