Information used for an enquiry

The form asks for your name, email address, phone number, preferred contact method, postcode, a short enquiry and confirmation that you have read this notice.

This information is used to receive your appointment request, respond using your preferred contact method, check whether the postcode is within the home-visit area and discuss availability. Please do not include detailed or urgent medical information in the form.

Strictly necessary website data

A secure session identifier supports the form’s security token and status messages. The rate limit uses a protected identifier, a window start time and a request count to reduce repeated automated submissions; it does not contain the enquiry itself.

The proposed retention criteria for enquiry, session and rate-limit information remain draft and require approval before this notice can be published.
