Help with breathing

Respiratory physiotherapy at home

I offer specialist respiratory physiotherapy for adults recovering from chest infections or pneumonia, and those living with bronchiectasis or other chronic respiratory conditions.

Your treatment is tailored to your needs and may include breathing control techniques, breathing exercises and gentle, graded physical activity. Together, we’ll work towards managing breathlessness, improving exercise tolerance and building confidence in everyday activities.

My aim is to help you feel more comfortable with your breathing and more confident managing your recovery or ongoing condition—in the comfort of your own home.
