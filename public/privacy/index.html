What information is collected and why

You provide the information directly. The form asks for your name, email address, phone number, preferred contact method, postcode, a short enquiry and confirmation that you have read this notice.

This information is used to receive and respond to your appointment request, use your preferred contact method, check whether the address is within the home-visit area and discuss availability. Please do not include detailed or urgent medical information in the form.

Your name, preferred contact method, postcode, short enquiry and privacy acknowledgement are required, together with either an email address or phone number. Without these details Melanie may be unable to respond or decide whether a home visit can be offered. There is no statutory requirement to provide this information, and submitting the form does not confirm an appointment.

Lawful basis

Contact and enquiry information is used because it is necessary to take steps at your request before potentially arranging physiotherapy services. The lawful basis is Article 6(1)(b) of the UK GDPR.

If your short enquiry includes information about your health, this is special category information. It is used only where necessary for the provision of health care or treatment under Article 9(2)(h) of the UK GDPR and Schedule 1, Part 1, paragraph 2 of the Data Protection Act 2018, subject to professional confidentiality.

Strictly necessary security information is used under Article 6(1)(f) of the UK GDPR. The legitimate interest is keeping the website and enquiry form secure, reliable and resistant to misuse.

Where information must be used or disclosed to comply with a legal obligation, the lawful basis is Article 6(1)(c) of the UK GDPR.

Where information is necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, the lawful basis is Article 6(1)(f) of the UK GDPR. The legitimate interest is protecting Melanie’s legal rights. If this use involves health information, the additional condition is Article 9(2)(f) of the UK GDPR.

The privacy checkbox confirms that you have read this notice. It is not consent to unrelated uses of your information.

Strictly necessary website data

A secure session identifier supports the form’s security token and status messages. It is stored in a cookie that expires when you close your browser. No advertising, analytics or other non-essential cookies are used.

To reduce repeated automated submissions, your internet address is converted into a protected identifier. The rate limit stores that identifier, a window start time and a request count, but not the enquiry itself. It is used for a one-hour window, and expired counter files are removed automatically during subsequent form activity.

The hosting service may also create technical server logs, such as the internet address, time and page requested, to operate and secure the website. These are kept only while needed for service operation, security and fault investigation under the hosting provider’s retention arrangements.

Who receives the information

Appointment enquiries are delivered to Melanie. GoDaddy processes website and server information as the hosting provider, and Titan processes the enquiry as the email provider. Their authorised service providers may also process information where necessary to supply, secure and support those services.

Information is not sold or used by Melanie for advertising or marketing. It may be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.

These providers and their authorised service providers may process information outside the UK, including in the United States. GoDaddy’s data processing terms say that transfers use UK adequacy regulations, including the UK-US Data Bridge where applicable, or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement for transfers not covered by adequacy. Titan’s public data processing terms say that standard data protection clauses under Article 46(2)(c) are incorporated where GDPR applies.

You may contact Melanie to request more information or a copy of the relevant safeguards.

How long information is kept

If an enquiry does not lead to treatment, the enquiry and related correspondence are kept for up to 12 months after the last contact and are then deleted.

If you become a patient, relevant correspondence may become part of your patient record. Adult patient records are normally kept for eight years after the last treatment, unless a longer period is needed to meet a legal or professional requirement.

Information may be kept for longer where this is necessary to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim or to comply with the law. Only relevant information will be retained, and it will be reviewed when the reason for keeping it ends.

Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to ask for access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, limits on its use or transfer of information you supplied.

Your right to object

You have the right to object to uses based on legitimate interests, including website security and the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. Whether an objection can be upheld depends on the circumstances and applicable law.

These rights are not always absolute. To make a request, email melanie@stronger-at-home.co.uk. Melanie may need to confirm your identity before responding.

No solely automated decision-making or profiling is used to decide whether physiotherapy services can be offered.
