# Brand messaging

## Core message

Experienced care. Personal progress. At home.

The voice is reassuring, hopeful and empowering. Lead with practical,
clinically credible reassurance for patients and older adults; adapt the
supporting detail for professional referrers without displacing the
patient-first message.

## Claims boundary

The approved claim is 20+ years of NHS experience. Do not promise or imply
guaranteed outcomes. HCPC, CSP, AGILE and ATOCP wording is verification-gated
and must not be presented as verified or public-facing until clearance records
confirm it. Public contact fields are also unresolved and must not be invented
or published.

The proposed public name is `Stronger at Home Physiotherapy`; the endorsement
is `by Melanie Watsham`. Do not use the name publicly before name clearance.
